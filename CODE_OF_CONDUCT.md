# Code of Conduct

## Our Goal
To maintain a respectful, professional, and efficient environment for everyone contributing to **Slimmer**.

## Expected Behavior
1. **Be Respectful**: Treat everyone with kindness. No personal attacks, trolling, or harassment.
2. **Be Constructive**: Focus on technical improvement. When giving feedback, stay objective and helpful.
3. **Be Inclusive**: Everyone is welcome, regardless of their background or level of experience.
4. **Be Direct**: Keep discussions related to the project and its goals (PDF optimization and PHP).

## Unacceptable Behavior
- Harassment or discrimination of any kind.
- Aggressive language or toxic behavior in issues and pull requests.
- Spamming or off-topic discussions.

## Enforcement
If you encounter any behavior that violates these rules, please report it by opening a private issue or contacting the maintainer at **isac.gondim.dev@gmail.com**. 

Violations may result in a warning or, in extreme cases, being blocked from the repository.

---
*By contributing to this project, you agree to abide by these simple rules.*
